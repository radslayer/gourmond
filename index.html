Gourmond

An experiment in building a marketing agent for food businesses.

What this is

Gourmond is a test bench. The idea is simple: caterers, meal-prep kitchens and other food businesses are good at food and short on time for marketing. This project explores how much of that marketing work an AI agent can carry.

What is being explored

Getting found

Simple, fast web pages that say clearly what a kitchen makes and who it is for.

Following up

Answering inquiries promptly and keeping in touch with people who asked for a quote.

Staying in touch

Regular, useful messages to past customers without adding work for the team.

Where it stands

Nothing here is a finished product or an offer. This page exists so the experiments have a home. It will change often.
